Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Bansal Coders ("we", "us") is a software development studio run by Shubham Bansal, based in Delhi NCR, India. Contact: bansalcoding500@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number (optional), company and project details. We do not collect payment card details on this website.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes, deliver and support the work you engage us for, and keep records required by Indian law. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Service providers

Contact-form messages are delivered through EmailJS and stored in our Google (Gmail) account. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Payments, when applicable, are processed by the payment provider named on your invoice.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts or scripts loaded from third-party CDNs may set technical data needed to serve them.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months; client project and billing records are kept as long as required for tax and legal purposes.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
